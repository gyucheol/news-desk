## 1. USD/KRW level and trend in Oct–Nov 2026 (incl. Nov 5–6), and how won moves have changed KRW returns on US ETFs

### Takeaway
The won went from crisis-level weakness (2026 high of 1,555.8 on Jul 2) to its strongest level in about two years (1,323.1 on Oct 26), a roughly 15% drop in USD/KRW. It then drifted back into the 1,340s in early November and jumped about 14 won to around 1,356 in the Nov 6 risk-off. The won has historically weakened in global selloffs, which softened KRW losses on unhedged US assets (2008, 2022). In H2 2026 the opposite risk showed up: the strengthening won wiped out about 15% of KRW value for anyone who bought unhedged in early July.

### Inferences
- **Nov 5–6 illustration (my calculation):** USD/KRW moved from 1,342.1 to 1,356.3 (+1.06%). On that basis:
  - SPY's −4.4% USD move becomes about **−3.4% in KRW**.
  - QQQ's −6.5% USD move becomes about **−5.5% in KRW**.
  - So the FX cushion in this episode is about 1 percentage point. That is far smaller than in 2008 or 2022, because exporter dollar sales capped the won's fall.
- **FX loss since July (my calculation):** 1,323.1 / 1,555.8 − 1 = **−15.0%**. That is what FX alone did to the KRW value of an unhedged US ETF bought on Jul 2 and marked on Oct 26.
- **Two-sided risk at today's level:** at about 1,340–1,356 the won is near its strongest in two years.
  - Further appreciation (the high-1,200s scenario) would erode unhedged KRW returns.
  - Renewed global risk-off tends to weaken the won and cushion them.
  - A Korean investor can manage this by splitting FX conversion or purchases over time, or by using hedged (H) Korean-listed versions. Hedged versions remove both the cushion and the appreciation risk.
- **Concentration:** Korean investors already carry heavy semiconductor exposure through the Kospi (Samsung and SK Hynix led the Nov 6 crash). QQQ's tech and semiconductor tilt adds to that correlation more than SPY does. This is relevant to the SPY-vs-QQQ choice in KRW terms.

## 2. Current (2026) tax rules for Korean residents on US-listed ETFs, and 2025–2026 changes (RIA, FX-hedge incentive, other measures)

### Takeaway
- **Capital gains:** SPY/QQQ gains in a general account are taxed separately at **22% on annual net KRW gains above ₩2.5m**, with all overseas stocks netted together. Investors self-file each May (June 1 in 2026). These gains sit **outside** the ₩20m financial-income comprehensive-tax test, while dividends count toward it.
- **2026 incentives:** 2026 added two temporary measures to stabilize the won.
  - **RIA** (국내시장 복귀계좌): a 100/80/50% capital-gains deduction depending on sale date, ending Dec 31, 2026.
  - A **5% FX-hedge deduction**.
- **Who qualifies:** both measures apply only to overseas stocks held on **Dec 23, 2025**. Under RIA, any 2026 net purchases of overseas stocks *or Korean-listed overseas ETFs* reduce the deduction.
- **Extension:** I found no extension into 2027.

### Cited Findings
**Base rules (secondary sources; no NTS page could be opened)**
- **Rate and netting:** 22% = 20% national + 2% local tax, applied to annual net gains minus the ₩2.5m basic deduction. Gains and losses on all overseas stocks are netted, even across countries (US stocks, Japanese ETFs, Chinese shares). For example, a ₩10m gain plus an ₩8m loss gives ₩2m net, which is inside the deduction, so the tax is 0. — [WikiDocs 2026 guide](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@Insight_Lab/22185/) (snippet); [KB Think overseas-stock tax page](https://kbthink.com/stock/foreign-tax.html) (snippet)
- **Worked example:** a net gain of ₩8m gives (₩8m − ₩2.5m) × 22% ≈ **₩1.21m**. — [Brunch](https://brunch.co.kr/@feel2174/108) (snippet)
- **Basic deduction:** it does not carry forward if unused. It is applied once per year across domestic (taxable) and overseas stock gains. — [GlassWallet FAQ](https://glasswallet.com/calculate/stock-capital-gains/faq), [k-calc](https://k-calc.com/calculator/stock-tax) (snippets; low authority)
- **Filing:** in May of the following year. The 2026 deadline was **June 1** (May 31 was a Sunday). — [GlassWallet 2026](https://glasswallet.com/calculate/stock-capital-gains/overseas) (snippet; low authority)
- **Classification:** gains on overseas-listed ETFs are capital gains (양도소득). They are not part of financial-income comprehensive taxation; losses are netted and the ₩2.5m deduction applies, but the 22% rate is higher than 15.4%. When interest plus dividends exceed **₩20m a year**, the excess is added to comprehensive income at progressive rates. — [Mirae Asset 투자와연금센터](https://investpension.miraeasset.com/contents/view.do?idx=15257), [KB Think ETF 세금](https://kbthink.com/etf/etf-tax.html) (snippets)

**RIA (국내시장 복귀계좌), new in 2026, temporary**
- **Announced** Dec 24, 2025 as an FX-stabilization measure. — [Segye, 2025-12-24](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251224512013)
- **Ministry outline (재정경제부 세제실 금융세제과, Jan 20, 2026):**
  - Sell overseas stocks, convert the proceeds to KRW and keep them in domestic listed stocks or domestic equity funds for 1 year.
  - Limit: **₩50m of sale proceeds per person**.
  - Net purchases of overseas stocks in a general account reduce the benefit proportionally.
  - Initial plan: 100% deduction for Q1, 80% for Q2, 50% for H2.
  - Related items: FX-hedge product deduction of **5%, capped at ₩5m**. Overseas-subsidiary dividend exclusion raised **95%→100%**. 국민참여형 국민성장펀드: 9% separate dividend tax (3+ year hold, ₩200m contribution cap) and an income deduction of up to 40%.
  - The overseas-stock measures are "올해에만 한시적으로 적용".
  - — [korea.kr policy news, 2026-01-20](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148958323)
- **Legislation and launch:**
  - Passed the National Assembly finance committee as a "1년 한시" special rule. — [Tax Times](https://www.taxtimes.co.kr/mobile/article.html?no=274350)
  - The 100% window was extended from end-March to **end-May**. — [Herald Economy, 2026-03-16](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026031618210534191)
  - Launched **Mar 23, 2026**. — [Nate/news, 2026-03-23](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260323n34687)
  - Wikipedia gives the effective date as Mar 24. The February launch was delayed because the Assembly prioritized the 대미투자법. — [Wikipedia KO](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EA%B5%AD%EB%82%B4%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5_%EB%B3%B5%EA%B7%80%EA%B3%84%EC%A2%8C)
- **Decree terms (조특령 §93의12, draft as of Mar 17, 2026):**
  - Eligible holdings: overseas stocks **held on Dec 23, 2025**, capped at that day's quantity.
  - Deduction by sale date: **through May 31: 100%; through Jul 31: 80%; through Dec 31: 50%**.
  - Cap: ₩50m across all accounts combined.
  - Holding period: 1 year.
  - Clawback if principal is withdrawn within 1 year: the reduced tax plus a **0.022%/day** surcharge. Exceptions cover disaster, death, or 3+ months of hospitalization.
  - Claimed on the **2026 capital-gains return, filed in 2027**.
  - — [Tax Times, 2026-03-17](https://www.taxtimes.co.kr/mobile/article.html?no=274353)
- **What counts as "해외주식등" when the deduction is cut:**
  - Overseas stocks **including overseas-listed ETFs**. — [Tax Times, 2026-03-17](https://www.taxtimes.co.kr/mobile/article.html?no=274353)
  - **Korean-issued overseas ETFs and ETNs** and Korean overseas equity funds. — [Tax Times, 2026-03-17](https://www.taxtimes.co.kr/mobile/article.html?no=274353); [KB Think RIA page, 2026-04-02](https://kbthink.com/investment/issues/ria.html)

  - Net purchases since Jan 1, 2026 are weighted **100% (Jan–May), 80% (Jun–Jul), 50% (Aug–Dec)**. — [KB Think RIA page, 2026-04-02](https://kbthink.com/investment/issues/ria.html)
  - Inside RIA, products with "해외 주식이 조금이라도 섞인" holdings do not qualify as domestic investments. — [KB Think RIA page, 2026-04-02](https://kbthink.com/investment/issues/ria.html)

**FX-hedge deduction (조특령 §93의13), 2026 only**
- **Decree terms (draft as of Mar 17, 2026):**
  - Mechanism: a resident signs a forward-sale (선물환 매도) contract with a broker.
  - Investment amount = forward amount × hedge days ÷ **275** (Apr 1–Dec 31, 2026).
  - Cap: the value of overseas stocks held on Dec 23, 2025.
  - Deduction: **5%, maximum ₩5m**, claimed on the 2026 return.
  - — [Tax Times, 2026-03-17](https://www.taxtimes.co.kr/mobile/article.html?no=274353); [korea.kr, 2026-01-20](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148958323)
- **Uptake:** about **₩100m** must be hedged to reach the ₩5m cap, and investors and brokers have shunned the product. — (snippet of [BizWatch, 2026-04-02](https://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/market/2026/04/02/0018))
- **Example product:** Daishin's forward-sale product is open only to individual professional investors and only at branches. It is an OTC derivative that can lose more than principal. Contracts completed by year-end earn the deduction (up to 5% of the average balance) on next year's overseas-stock and overseas-ETF capital-gains tax. — [Herald Economy, 2026-05-03](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026050318271957668)

### Inferences
- **RIA trap:** anyone who used RIA in 2026 loses part of the deduction by buying SPY/QQQ, *or* TIGER/KODEX S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100 ETFs (even inside pension or ISA), before Dec 31, 2026. Net purchases in this period are weighted 50%. If the RIA deduction is material, deferring those purchases to January 2027 avoids the cut. This assumes the adjustment covers calendar 2026 only, which the decree's filing rule ("2026 net purchases") implies.
- **Hedge deduction:** it is not a tool for a *new* SPY/QQQ position. It requires Dec 23, 2025 holdings, is practically limited to professional investors, and ends Dec 31, 2026.
- **Policy direction:** 2026 tax incentives (RIA, domestic-only ISA, 국민성장펀드) push toward domestic assets. None favor new overseas ETF purchases.
- **Tax-timing tool:** for US-listed ETFs, investors can harvest losses within the year, since netting spans all overseas stocks. They can also spread sales across years to use the ₩2.5m deduction each year.

  - The ban on loss carryforward.

  - The 2025 rule on selling shares within a year of a gift (이월과세).
- The final enacted decree text was not seen; the Tax Times version is the March 17 draft.

## 3. Largest Korean-listed S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 ETFs: fees, AUM, hedged vs unhedged, and general-account taxation

### Takeaway
The Korean-listed versions are very large and cheap.
- **TIGER 미국S&P500** (360750) had **₩21.0tn** on Oct 15, 2026, briefly Korea's largest ETF.
- **TIGER 미국나스닥100** (133690) had **₩11.9tn**.

- **Hedging and distributions:** ACE 미국나스닥100 is unhedged; hedged "(H)" versions are offered for investors who want FX hedging. TIGER 미국나스닥100 pays distributions quarterly. — (snippets, blog-level)
- **General-account tax:**
  - Sale gains are taxed at **15.4% on the lesser of the increase in 과표기준가 and the actual trading gain**, withheld at sale.
  - Distributions are also taxed at 15.4%.
  - **No loss netting:** a ₩10m gain on one ETF and a ₩2.5m loss on another still gives ₩1.54m tax.
  - These gains are dividend income, so they **count toward the ₩20m comprehensive-taxation threshold**.
  - — [KB Think ETF 세금](https://kbthink.com/etf/etf-tax.html), [Samsung KODEX ETF tax guide](https://www.samsungfund.com/etf/insight/guide/view05.do), [Mirae Asset 투자와연금센터](https://investpension.miraeasset.com/contents/view.do?idx=15257) (snippets)
- **2025 change in the foreign tax credit:** since 2025, Korean funds receive overseas dividends net of foreign tax, with no upfront refund. This created **double taxation in pension accounts** when the money is withdrawn. — [SBS Biz, 2025-02-05](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000216207); [KB Think, 2025-08-29](https://kbthink.com/pension/plan/gw/250829-2.html)
  - Price gains are unaffected; only foreign-withheld dividends are. — (snippet of [Mirae Asset 투자와연금센터](https://investpension.miraeasset.com/contents/view.do?idx=23438))

### Inferences
- **Same index, two currencies:** the SPY-vs-QQQ decision can be carried out in USD (SPY/QQQ) or in KRW (TIGER/KODEX 미국S&P500 vs 미국나스닥100), because the indexes are the same. The unhedged Korean versions carry the same USD/KRW exposure.
- **Break-even (my calculation, general account, ignoring FX costs):**
  - US-listed tax = 22% × (G − ₩2.5m). Korean-listed tax = 15.4% × G.
  - These are equal at **G ≈ ₩8.33m** of annual realized gains.
  - Below that, US-listed is cheaper. Above it, Korean-listed is cheaper.
  - The Korean-listed advantage disappears if ETF gains push financial income over ₩20m (progressive comprehensive tax). US-listed also allows loss netting and choosing which year to realize gains.
- **Trading hours:** Korean-listed versions trade during Seoul hours, in KRW, with no FX conversion by the investor. On Nov 6 a Korean investor could act on the US selloff during the Korean day. Prices then reflect US futures and FX, and premiums or discounts to iNAV can widen.

- Tracking differences were not checked.
- SPY's and QQQ's own expense ratios were not verified here (other notes may cover them).

## 4. ISA and pension accounts (연금저축, IRP): benefits, limits, 2025–2026 reforms, and whether US-listed ETFs can be held

### Takeaway
US-listed SPY/QQQ **cannot** be held in ISA, 연금저축 or IRP. Korean-listed S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 ETFs **can**, and these accounts are where the Korean versions clearly win.
- **연금저축/IRP:** 13.2–16.5% tax credit on up to ₩9m a year, tax deferred until withdrawal, then 3.3–5.5% pension tax.
- **ISA:** ₩2m tax-free (₩4m low-income) and 9.9% above that, with loss netting inside the account and a 3-year minimum.
- **Reforms:** a fix for double taxation of foreign dividends applies to pension withdrawals from Jul 1, 2026. Proposed ISA expansions are unconfirmed, and the 2026 plan's new ISA is domestic-only.

### Cited Findings
- **Pension tax credit:**
  - 연금저축: up to **₩6m**; **₩9m** combined with IRP.
  - Rate: **16.5%** for total salary ≤ ₩55m, otherwise 13.2% (some sources quote the national-tax-only 15%/12%).
  - Maximum refund: about **₩1.485m**.
  - Payments made by Dec 31 count for that year's 연말정산.
  - — (snippet of [Daum news, 2025-11-19 "'600·300' 기억하고 148만원 돌려받으세요"](https://v.daum.net/v/20251119063226473)); [WikiDocs TaxSave 2026](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@TaxSave/32004/) (blog)
- **Pension taxation:**
  - Gains and distributions are untaxed until withdrawal.
  - Pension receipts are taxed at **3.3–5.5% by age**.
  - Non-pension withdrawal or early termination is taxed at **16.5%** (other income, separately taxed).
  - An older source says that if pension receipts funded by investment returns exceed **₩15m a year**, the *whole* amount is comprehensively taxed (see Gaps).
  - — [Mirae Asset 투자와연금센터](https://investpension.miraeasset.com/contents/view.do?idx=15257), [KB Think](https://kbthink.com/main/economy/expert-column/tax-column/tax-column-230719.html) (snippets)
- **2025 tax-law amendment (pension):**
  - Foreign tax paid inside pension accounts is accumulated as a credit and deducted at pension receipt.
  - It covers indirect-investment income from **Jan 1, 2025**, applied to **withdrawals on or after Jul 1, 2026**. Withdrawals in the gap period remain double-taxed.
  - The lifetime-annuity separate tax rate falls from 4% to **3%**.
  - The source describes the *proposal*.
  - — [KB Think, 2025-08-29](https://kbthink.com/pension/plan/gw/250829-2.html)
  - Earlier reporting said the ministry would fix the 연금저축/IRP problem via the July 2025 revision, effective the following year. — [SBS Biz, 2025-02-05](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000216207)
- **ISA (existing):**
  - Tax-free **₩2m** (general) or **₩4m** (서민형/농어민); **9.9% separate tax** on the excess.
  - **3-year** minimum.
  - — (snippets of multiple 2026 guides, e.g., [WikiDocs](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@infoplant/20949/))
  - The 2026 reform plan keeps the existing ISA's annual contribution at **₩20m**, keeps the allowance, and extends the sign-up period to end-2029. — [MTN, 2026-08-03](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026080310261915519)
- **ISA expansion claims (conflict):** some blogs say ISA limits rose to **₩40m a year (₩200m total)** and the tax-free amount to **₩5m/₩10m** in 2026. — [Daum/blog](https://v.daum.net/v/2rSbOAAifi), [thecheck](https://thecheck.co.kr/?p=4679)
  - **Contradicted** by MTN's Aug 2026 report that the existing ISA limit is unchanged, and by a guide noting the proposal was "not finalized". Treat the expansion as unenacted.
- **생산적금융 ISA (proposed Aug 3, 2026):**
  - Eligible assets: domestic listed stocks, domestic equity funds, 국민성장펀드 and BDCs.
  - **Full tax exemption** on interest and dividends.
  - Contributions: ₩20m a year, ₩200m total.
  - Term: 3-year mandatory, extendable up to 10 years.
  - Excludes anyone subject to comprehensive financial-income tax in any of the prior 3 years.
  - A youth version adds a 10% income deduction.
  - Maturity money can move to a pension account, with 10% counting toward the extra pension credit limit.
  - — [MTN, 2026-08-03](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026080310261915519)
- **What can be held:** overseas directly listed ETFs are excluded from IRP, while Korean-listed overseas-index ETFs are allowed. The domestic-investment ISA type excludes overseas ETFs. — (snippet, blog-level: [WikiDocs TaxSave](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@TaxSave/32004/))
- **RIA interaction:** overseas ETF purchases inside 연금저축, IRP and ISA count as overseas net purchases for the RIA adjustment. — [KB Think RIA page, 2026-04-02](https://kbthink.com/investment/issues/ria.html)

### Inferences
- **Order of accounts for a long-horizon investor in KRW:**
  1. Fill 연금저축/IRP (the 13.2–16.5% upfront credit beats any fee or tax difference) with Korean-listed S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100 ETFs.
  2. Then ISA (₩2m/₩4m tax-free plus 9.9%, with in-account loss netting).
  3. Then the general account. Choose US-listed SPY/QQQ for large gains, loss harvesting or investors near ₩20m of financial income; choose Korean-listed for smaller gains.
- **2026 timing:** for RIA users, new overseas-ETF buying inside these accounts in Nov–Dec 2026 also cuts the RIA deduction.
- **Dividend drag:** the double-tax issue matters more for higher-yield products than for S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100 ETFs, whose yields are low (yield levels not verified here).

### Gaps
- **₩15m pension threshold:** the 2024 rule letting taxpayers choose **16.5% separate taxation** instead of comprehensive taxation is not verified; the source found may describe pre-2024 law.
- **Other unverified pension and ISA rules:**
  - The IRP 70% risky-asset cap and how equity-only overseas-index ETFs are treated under it.
  - The existing ISA total cap (₩100m).
  - The ISA→pension transfer credit cap.
  - Pension receipt conditions (age 55+, 10 years).
- **Double-tax fix:** whether it was enacted exactly as proposed, and whether it extends to ISA, was not verified.
- **NTS/FSS:** no official FSS or NTS guidance pages were retrieved.

## 5. Practical costs: FX conversion spreads, brokerage fees, trading hours

### Takeaway
Only blog-level 2026 sources were found. For US-listed SPY/QQQ, FX conversion (spread and preferential-rate terms, which vary by broker and time of day) usually costs more than the commission. Promotional US-stock commissions of about 0.03–0.07% are common, and Meritz reportedly offers free trading and USD conversion through end-2026. Korean-listed ETFs avoid investor-side FX conversion and overnight US trading hours.

### Cited Findings
- **FX vs commissions:** the real costs of US stocks are FX spreads and capital-gains tax, not commissions. Conversion timing can change FX cost by up to 10×. — [WikiDocs blog, 2026](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@studylibrary/19923/) (low authority)
- **Preferential rates:** they vary with market conditions. Non-real-time or night-time conversion can apply unfavorable rates. — [WikiDocs blogs, 2026](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@infoplant/20018/) (snippet; low authority)
- **Broker promotions:** Meritz offers free domestic and overseas trading and USD conversion until end-2026 (promotion; check conditions). Promotional commissions run about **0.03–0.07%**. Investors should compare FX preferential rates, regulatory fees, promotion periods and conditions, and available markets. — [WikiDocs blog comparing 키움·토스·미래에셋, 2026](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@infoplant/20020/) (snippet; low authority)
- **RIA conversion:** RIA sale proceeds are converted to KRW automatically on the settlement date. — [KB Think RIA page, 2026-04-02](https://kbthink.com/investment/issues/ria.html)
- **Korean FX market:** Korean news reports quote a "주간 거래 종가" (daytime close, 15:30) separately from other closes, which implies an overnight session as well. — [Aju News, 2026-11-06](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20261106102358104)

### Inferences
- **US hours (my calculation):** US daylight saving time ended Sunday, Nov 1, 2026. The US regular session (09:30–16:00 ET) now runs **23:30–06:00 KST**; under DST it was 22:30–05:00.
- **Korean hours:** Korean-listed ETFs trade in Seoul regular hours (09:00–15:30 KST, general knowledge, not verified here).
- **Round-trip cost for US-listed:** two FX conversions plus commission. If a broker gives a 90–95% preferential rate on a ~1% posted spread, each conversion costs about 0.05–0.1%. This is an illustrative assumption, not a sourced figure.
- **Korean-listed costs:** a KRW commission plus the bid-ask spread and any premium or discount to iNAV. The fund's internal FX and trading costs show up in 실부담비용 (~0.1%; see Q3).
