# The lesser-known sims in SubTwoFifty's lower tiers (C–F): what each one is, how it is rated, and whether his one-line reasons hold

All checks were made on 2026-11-11. Live numbers (prices, review summaries, current players, Roblox stats) were taken between 07:30 and 07:45 UTC, which is 16:30–16:45 KST and 02:30 US Eastern, an off-peak hour for the Americas.

The creator's verdicts are quoted from `reel_tier_list.md` in this folder; they come from the [ranking reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfkss5XqOJt/) of 2026-11-10.

Labels used below:
- "Steam reviews" means user reviews on Steam (community evidence).
- "Expert" means a recognised FPV reviewer (Oscar Liang, Joshua Bardwell).
- Dates without a year on Steam review cards are 2026, because Steam leaves out the current year.
- YouTube dates are the relative ages that YouTube search showed on 2026-11-11, so they are approximate.

## 1. Overview: do the creator's lower-tier placements hold up?

### Takeaway
For most of the 12 entries, his one-line reasons match the evidence:
- **FPVSIM.com (F):** the lowest-rated of everything checked, at 16% positive on Steam.
- **Endless FPV (E):** only two themed decks at launch, and reviewers call it repetitive and floaty.
- **Overfly (D):** its own developer posted a "how to fix low FPS" video, and the site was down for maintenance on 2026-11-11.
- **EreaDrone (D):** it is 3.4 years old, and the developer itself calls its 2026 work the "Road to 1.0".
- **WebFPV (C):** Oscar Liang also notes "odd collisions".

Some claims cannot be tested or verified:
- Killzone FPV's "nobody's playing"
- the Roblox "paywalled settings"
- the Festival sim "gifted by the dev" note
- the identity of "Intevi FPV Sim", which I could not find

Expert and community opinion differs most from his letters for three programs:
- **WebFPV:** Oscar Liang features it in his main sim list and calls it "incredibly impressive" as a free, instant-entry sim, so C undersells it for a beginner.
- **Firehawk:** 91% of 536 Steam reviews are positive, so players like it far more than a C suggests. C is still fair if the question is training value.
- **EreaDrone:** Oscar Liang's table describes it as having "beginner-friendly physics", and it hosts official FAI e-Drone Racing World Cup rounds. D is defensible from current user reviews (Mixed, 56%), but it is harsher than how experts frame it.

## 2. FPV Festival Simulator (C): "Train to fly festivals. A unique and interesting sim."

### Takeaway
- **What it is.** "Festival FPV" is a free browser sim for show flying: a cinewhoop over festival stages with lasers, pyro and a crowd. It is made by Big Sky FPV, an aerial production company that says it flies events "nationwide" (its country is not stated), and its chief pilot Jason El Moussa.
- **Price.** Two venues are free forever. All eleven venues cost a one-time US$9.99.
- **Unique.** The claim holds: I found no other festival-flying sim.
- **Coverage.** No expert has covered it, and its YouTube views are tiny.
- **Placement.** C fits a niche, novel, but lightly reviewed program.
- **"Gifted by the dev".** This could not be verified. If true, the gift would be the $9.99 unlock.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Firehawk (C): "Firehawk, a wicked time, but not for learning how to fly."

- **What it is.** "Firehawk: FPV Drone Shooter" by TruePlayers, formerly "Nexus FPV".
- **Release, price and language.** Released 2025-08-22; ₩21,500; supports Korean.
- **Ratings and players.**
  - Steam users rate it Very Positive (91% of 536; recent 80% of 15).
  - Its player base is small (all-time peak 71; about 15 average in the last 30 days).
- **"Wicked time".** Clearly supported.
- **"Not for learning how to fly".** Broadly fair: it is a combat roguelike with no lessons, and it is absent from Oscar Liang's sim list. But it uses FPV acro-style physics with real-radio support, and some players say it improved their flying.
- **Placement.** C is low for a well-loved game, but defensible as a training sim.

## 5. WebFPV (C): "A free browser sim with full Betaflight configurator, but the crashes were a mess."

### Takeaway
- **What it is.** WebFPV (webfpv.org) is a free, no-install, no-account browser sim. It runs real Betaflight 4.5.1 compiled to WebAssembly and accepts a USB radio or gamepad.
- **Steam version.** "webFPV" (developer Mathew Harvey, publisher andAgainSoftware) is listed as coming in November 2026. Its listing describes 73 lessons in 16 chapters and offline play. It is unclear whether the browser version already has the same lessons.
- **The creator's two claims.** The real-Betaflight part is supported. His crash complaint matches Oscar Liang's "odd collisions… floatiness".
- **Placement.** Oscar features WebFPV in his main list and calls it "incredibly impressive" as a free entry point, and several YouTubers covered it in October–November 2026. So C looks harsh for a beginner-oriented free tool, though defensible on physics.

### Cited Findings
- **Site.** Title "WebFPV: Free Browser FPV Simulator Online | Real Betaflight 4.5". Description: "Real Betaflight 4.5.1 compiled to WebAssembly. No download, no account. Fly with a radio or game controller."
  - It is funded by tips and Patreon: "Your tip helps keep WebFPV running. The simulator stays free…"
  - It has a track builder and a whoop "Micro UTT" mode. — [webfpv.org](https://webfpv.org/)
- **Steam listing (app 4944190).** — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4944190/); [Steam appdetails](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=4944190&cc=kr&l=korean)
  - Developer Mathew Harvey, publisher andAgainSoftware.
  - Status "coming soon" with date "November 2026". No price is shown yet, but the listing is not marked free.
  - English only, Windows. No user reviews.
  - The description says it "compiles the open-source flight controller firmware most racing quads run" and that "Rates, PIDs, filters, feedforward, anti-gravity, and airmode are the firmware's own".
  - "Learn 73 lessons in 16 chapters", from "First Hover … in Angle mode" to "Switching to Acro" and a "Pro Licence".
  - "A manga styled drone sim that runs fully offline."
  - It recommends "A gamepad or an FPV radio in USB joystick mode", and needs only integrated graphics with WebGL 2.
- **Expert (Oscar Liang).** — [Oscar Liang](https://oscarliang.com/fpv-simulator/)
  - "You go to their website in your browser, plug in your radio via USB, and start flying. There is no download, no account…"
  - "In game, you can change rates, PID settings, camera angle, FOV and battery parameters…"
  - "The downside is that the physics are still a work in progress. There can be some odd collisions and occasional simulator 'floatiness'…"
  - "I would not replace VelociDrone, Liftoff or Uncrashed with WebFPV just yet, but as a free, instant-entry FPV simulator, it is incredibly impressive."
- **YouTube coverage (community; relative ages, views on 2026-11-11).**
  - Squadding Quads, "This FREE FPV Simulator Runs in Your Browser… But Is It Any Good?" (about 1 month, 21,035 views). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-LyXljbRh9E)
  - CAPTAIN DRONE, "FREE FPV Drone Simulator - WEBFPV" (about 2 weeks, 28,541 views). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPOBO0pPt_A)
  - Nick Burns, "FREE - No Install - FPV Simulator - WebFPV" (about 7 days, 14,143 views; snippet: "I don't SIM often but when I do I use WEBFPV.org"). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gx0lfAhQnMg)
  - FpvPreach, "Live review, WebFpv.org" (streamed about 7 days ago). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HweU-YFdjLg)

### Inferences
- "Full Betaflight configurator" is loosely worded. What WebFPV runs is the Betaflight firmware and its tuning parameters, not the Betaflight Configurator desktop app. The substance of the claim (real Betaflight tuning) is right.
- His crash verdict dates from no later than 29 September (it was on the Part 9 list), and the sim is updated quickly. Collision behaviour may have changed since then; that is unverified.
- **For a Korean beginner:** this is among the most useful items on the whole lower half of the list. It is free, instant and works with a radio, and the Steam listing describes a structured curriculum from first hover to acro. The drawbacks are English only and imperfect collisions.

### Gaps
- The Steam price and exact release day are unknown.
