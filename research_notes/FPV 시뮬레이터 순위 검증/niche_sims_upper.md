## 1. What is each program? (developer, platform, price, status, last update, controller, Korean)

### Takeaway
All six were identified, five with high confidence and Drone Party with medium-high confidence.

- **Four are paid Steam titles** (Korean store prices):
  - The Zone ₩15,500
  - FPV LOGIC ₩21,500
  - FPV Labs ₩24,500
  - CarX Drift Racing Online ₩16,500, plus a free third-party mod
- **Two are free browser sims:** Flight Division and Drone Party.
- **Status and team size:** apart from CarX, all are early-access or alpha projects made by one or two people.
- **Korean:** only CarX has Korean.
- **Controllers:** all five standalone sims take a USB RC radio or a gamepad. Whether KiNO's drone accepts an RC radio could not be confirmed.

### Inferences
- **Paid titles are a bet.** All four standalone sims are early-access or alpha, and all are made by one or two people. Paying for one means betting the developer keeps going. FPV LOGIC shows the downside: its own v1.0 target ("within 2024") was missed, and Steam now flags it as not updated for over 17 months.
- **Lowest-cost entry for the beginner.** For a Korean beginner practising at home, the two browser sims cost nothing and run on whatever device is at hand, even a keyboard. Flight Division is the only one of the six built around step-by-step lessons.
- **Language.** None of the five actual drone sims has a Korean interface. CarX does, but its drone is a mod feature, not a sim.

## 2. How do Steam users rate them, and how many play?

### Takeaway
- **The Zone is the clear standout:** 97% of 487 reviews, recently 98% of 56, and a player peak at its all-time high.

### Inferences
- **The Zone.** 20 months after launch its reviews keep coming in and its daily peak has just hit an all-time high. The audience is growing, not fading.
- **FPV LOGIC.** Recent reviews are still mostly positive. Players like the existing build even though development has stalled.
- **FPV Labs.** The "Mixed" score is mostly launch-week reviews, written before two physics reworks, so it may understate the current build. But the few later reviews are mostly negative, and 0 players were online at check. There is no evidence of a turnaround.
- **Who writes these reviews.** Steam reviewers of these titles are mostly existing hobbyists with radios. High scores show that pilots like the feel. They do not show the sims suit someone who has never flown.

### Gaps
- **CarX all-language total.** The store page shows only the English-language figure. Steam's app metadata reports "recommendations: 83000". The blocked appreviews endpoint would have given the exact all-language number.

## 6. Are the A/B placements reasonable, especially for a Korean beginner practising at home?

### Takeaway
- **The Zone (A)** has the strongest evidence of the six.
- **Supported:** FPV Logic (B) and Flight Division (B) are reasonable.
- **Rest on taste:** Drone Party (A) and CarX + KiNO (B) depend on the creator's enjoyment, with no independent evidence behind them.
- **Looks generous:** FPV Labs (B), given its Mixed reviews and empty servers.
- **The list is not a beginner ranking.** For a beginner, the free Flight Division lessons and The Zone's well-reviewed, low-spec trainer are the best supported of these six.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **The Zone, A: reasonable and well supported.** It has the best user ratings of the six, active solo development, and realism praised by Oscar Liang and forum users. One commenter even argues for S.
  - For a beginner it is cheap and runs on modest PCs. But it needs a radio or gamepad, has an English-only interface, and is explicitly a training tool, not a casual game.
- **Drone Party, A: a fun pick, not an evidence-backed sim ranking.** His description is accurate, but no expert or community evaluation exists, and the flight model's realism is unknown.
  - As a free, no-install first taste with a tutorial and Angle mode it suits a beginner. An A for "sim quality" is unverifiable.
- **FPV Logic, B: reasonable.** Players and Dronarchy like how it flies, but development has stalled, and Oscar Liang calls it barebones.
  - For a beginner, paying ₩21,500 for a stalled early-access game is the riskier choice. There are also macOS 27 controller reports with no developer fix.
- **CarX + KiNO, B: only reasonable as entertainment.** It is a mod inside a driving game, not an FPV trainer. A beginner should not buy CarX in order to learn FPV.
- **Flight Division, B: reasonable, and for a beginner arguably more useful than its letter suggests.** It is the only one with a structured lesson path, and it is free with no install.
  - Caveats: an e-mail account is needed, optional purchases exist, there is no Korean, and only one independent review was found.
- **FPV Labs, B: generous on current evidence.** His specific points (sound, missing features) are accurate. But the community's Mixed rating, contested physics, the highest price, heavier hardware needs and almost no players make it the weakest-supported placement of the six. It may improve; it is early access with physics reworks.
- **Overall.** His upper tiers mix "best-feeling sim for a freestyler" (The Zone, FPV Logic, FPV Labs) with "most fun or most novel" (Drone Party, CarX + KiNO). The letters should not be read as advice for someone who has never flown.
