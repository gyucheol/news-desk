# Buying a first FPV radio in Korea (November 2026): sellers, prices, stock, extras, KC/전파법 and overseas purchase

Research date: 2026-11-11. All pages were accessed on 2026-11-11, in the evening KST (from about 20:50 KST; the first server response was stamped 11:49 GMT). Legend:
- **(page)** means I read the product page's own HTML.
- **(list)** means I read the shop's category or search list only.
- **(snippet only)** means I saw the fact only in a search-result summary and did not open the page. Treat it as weaker.

How stock was read: most shops run on Cafe24. On those I read the page's own markers: the `is_soldout_icon` flag, the 품절 icon, whether the 구매하기/바로구매/BUY IT NOW button or the SOLD OUT button is visible or hidden (`displaynone`), and the per-option stock data. **"Orderable"** means the page showed an active buy button and no sold-out marker. It does not prove the goods are on a shelf. Several shops do not track stock at all (`use_stock: false`), or show "∞". Prices are list prices for 일반 (non-member) buyers.

### Inferences
- **Prices are effectively fixed nationwide**, which suggests importer-set pricing. The KC units at these shops trace to one importer, 수성RC (see Q3). Pocket ELRS is 129,000원 everywhere, Pocket CC2500 109,000원 and Boxer Crush 355,000원. TX12 MK2 CC2500 is the exception: 169,000원 at 엑스캅터 and 링크드론 versus 189,000원 at 수성RC and 퍼스트알씨.

Turning them into ELRS needs a separate 49,000원 module, and I did not verify that a swapped module keeps KC status. That makes them 218,000–248,000원 in total, against 129,000원 for the Pocket ELRS.
- The cheapest orderable domestic Pocket ELRS route is 링크드론 or 퍼스트알씨 (free or low shipping) or 수성RC (+3,500원). 엑스캅터 is out of stock. 드론몰 is orderable by markup, but its page makes no KC claim.

- The DJI FPV Remote Controller 3 (209,000원) only makes sense for a DJI goggle system. The Tango 2 is unavailable, and LiteRadio is effectively unavailable domestically. Both fall back to 직구 (Q4).

## Q2. What else a beginner must buy to use the radio, with Korean prices

### Takeaway
The Pocket comes **without batteries**. It needs **two 18650 Li-ion cells**, which Korean shops sell for 5,800–7,700원 each. No shop bundles cells by default: 링크드론 offers them as a 5,800원 add-on option, and the others sell them as separate items. A **USB-C cable is in the box** and the radio **charges its cells internally over USB-C**, so a phone charger is enough and no separate charger is needed.
- **Zorro:** comes with two 18350 cells, so nothing extra is needed.
- **TX12 MK2 and Boxer:** include an 18650 tray and a USB-C cable but no cells.

No extra USB-C cable is needed either, because one is in every RadioMaster box listed. The Pocket spec lists firmware upgrades "Via USB", which implies the included cable carries data. ## Q3. Certification and import rules: KC 적합성평가, checking a seller's KC claim, and the personal-use 직구 exemption

### Takeaway
Anyone who **manufactures, sells or imports** a 2.4 GHz RC transmitter in Korea needs **적합성평가** under 전파법 제58조의2. The RadioMaster radios sold domestically are registered as "특정소출력 무선기기(무선데이터통신시스템용 무선기기)" with **적합등록** status (numbers starting R-R-).
- **All five KC numbers quoted by the shops match records** in the 국립전파연구원 database, held by the importer 수성알씨(수성RC). Two other importers hold separate Boxer ELRS registrations, and 헬셀 holds a second Pocket record.
- **No KC record was found** for BETAFPV LiteRadio or TBS radios.
- **An individual may bring in one unit for personal use without 적합성평가** (전파법 제58조의3제1항제1호; 시행령 별표 6의2 제1호 자목). They **may not sell it within one year** of import.
- KC-version Pockets and Boxers ship with **Korea-specific ELRS firmware**. Receivers, including a future whoop's, must be flashed to match before they will bind.

### Inferences
- **The KC claims on the domestic Pocket ELRS, Pocket CC2500, TX12 MK2, Zorro, Boxer and Boxer Crush are genuine.** Each number the shops print matches a live RRA record. The shops' identical wording points to units imported by 수성RC; the KC sticker is said to be inside the rear module cover. A buyer can confirm by entering the number in the RRA search with dates left blank.
- **A KC number belongs to its registrant's units, not to the model in general.** 드론파일럿 and 디스페이스 each registered the same "BOXER ELRS" model separately. So a parallel-imported or 직구 unit of the same model is not covered by 수성RC's registration. A Korean shop selling a non-KC unit would breach 전파법 제58조의2. 드론몰's "ELRS, FCC" Pocket page makes no KC claim, and whether its stock is a 수성RC unit is unknown.
- **For simulator use, the Korean firmware is irrelevant**, since USB joystick mode does not transmit. **For a later whoop it matters a lot:** shops say a KC Pocket or Boxer binds only to receivers flashed with the matching Korean ("KC-domain") ELRS build. Most imported BNF whoops ship with standard ELRS firmware, so a beginner would have to reflash the whoop's receiver over a wired connection. 엑스캅터 also warns that putting standard firmware on a KC Boxer can damage its attenuator. This is a real trade-off against buying abroad (Q4).
- **직구 of one radio for personal use is legal** under the exemption. A conservative reading of "1대" is that importing a second transmitter in the same consignment falls outside it. Selling a 직구 radio within one year of import is prohibited.
- Using a non-KC FCC radio in Korea at its default ELRS power may exceed the Korean 특정소출력 limit. The KC versions were explicitly fitted with attenuators to comply. A 직구 buyer would be prudent to run the lowest power setting, but compliance is unverified (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- The law and decree text were not read on law.go.kr. Article numbering relies on the RRA page (전파법 제58조의2, 제58조의3제1항제1호; "시행령 제77조의7 별표6의2") and the 법제처 interpretation ("별표 6의2 제1호자목"). The effective date of the "1년" proviso was not found.
- No official source found says whether an individual must file anything to use the one-unit exemption. The RRA page lists the exemption but not a procedure; its phone line for 적합성평가 면제 is 031-644-7533.
- The penalty amounts for selling uncertified equipment were not verified; RRA says only "규정에 따라 처벌".
- No 관세청 source was found on whether a personally imported transmitter needs any 세관장 확인 or is excluded from 목록통관.
- The Korean 2.4 GHz power limit and the 2026-9호 amendment are snippet-level only. Whether a stock FCC ELRS radio operated in Korea complies with the 신고 없이 개설할 수 있는 무선국 rules is unresolved.
- The DJI FPV Remote Controller 3's KC record was not found. Its model number was unknown, and a maker query for "SZ DJI" over 2024-01-01–2024-04-30 returned no rows (likely a query problem).
- The "KC-domain" ELRS branch could not be checked on GitHub (no access from this session). Its existence rests on the 엑스캅터 page only.
- Whether swapping in the 49,000원 ELRS module on a KC Zorro or TX12 keeps KC status was not checked.

## Q4. Overseas direct purchase as a fallback: price, shipping and customs points

### Takeaway
RadioMaster's official store lists the **Pocket ELRS at $71.50, about 95,960원** at 1,342.1원/USD, with every variant in stock. That compares with 129,000원 in Korea, before international shipping, which is shown only at checkout.
- **BETAFPV LiteRadio 3 / LiteRadio 4:** $32.99 / $44.99, plus $5 shipping, which is about 51,000원 / 67,100원. 직구 is the only real route to them.
