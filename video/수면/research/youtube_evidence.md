# Sleep sub-angles — YouTube evidence check (for 「INFJ입니다.」)

Checked 2026-11-03 (UTC). 285 videos checked with the project checker (`node video/tools/youtube_views.js`, mattw.io YouTube Metadata = official YouTube Data API values).

## How this was done
- Discovery: WebSearch only (no YouTube page was fetched). Video IDs were taken from result URLs.
- Numbers: every viewCount / publishedAt / checkedAt / channel below is copied from the checker output (raw lines in `raw/batch*.jsonl`). Videos the checker could not read are listed as not usable.
- Threshold: long-form ≥ 500,000 views, Shorts ≥ 1,000,000 views (absolute; channel-median multiples were not computed).
- Format (Shorts vs long-form): from the search-result URL (`/shorts/`) or `#shorts` in the title; for every video ≥ 300k views (and the borderline ones) the duration was read from the same mattw.io page with a small read-only scratchpad helper (`helper/meta.js`, `helper/duration.js`; they do not read view counts). The YouTube API has no Shorts flag, so a video of 1–3 min without other signals is marked "probably Shorts" and, if it sits between 500k and 1M, treated conservatively as NOT meeting.
- † = the checker truncates titles that contain a `"` (regex limitation); full title read from the same mattw.io page by the helper.
- Relevance: direct = the video is about this sub-angle; adjacent = related (same symptom/situation but another framing, e.g. how-to-fall-asleep tips, dawn awakening); off = little relation.
- Korean videos first; English rows are secondary evidence only.

## Key observations & caveats
- **Sub-angle 1 (phone / staying up):** no Korean video about the *why* (보복성 수면 미루기, 보상심리) reached 500k. Closest on-topic: 보다 BODA "잠 들기 전에 핸드폰을 놓지 못하는 이유(도파민 중독;;)" 473,881 (long-form). Korean qualifiers are only adjacent and health-framed (지식인사이드 doctors on sleeping after 1 a.m.; 주은연 on bad sleep habits). English psychology narration (QuietStrength, 991,967) does qualify as secondary evidence.
- **Sub-angle 2 (sleep effort):** clearly proven demand, but dominated by doctors/hospitals and by "how to fall asleep fast" technique videos (1분만, 지식인사이드 이동환, 닥터딩요, 김소형). The on-topic qualifiers that fit the angle best are 비온뒤/신원철 "잠은 못잘수록 더 못잔다", 서울대병원tv/이유진 (fear of not sleeping) and 피지컬갤러리 (chapter "잠을 포기하면 오히려 잠이 온다") — all medical or lifestyle channels, not psychology channels.
- **Sub-angle 3 (emotions):** the only psychology-framed Korean qualifier is 세바시 김경일 "잠만 잘자도 성격 좋아진다" (1.23M). The other qualifiers frame sleep loss as body/brain damage (과학을 보다, 사물궁이, 박문호/BTN on Alzheimer's). Emotion- or relationship-specific videos stayed far below the line (김경일 방송대 "잘 자야 착해진다?" 190,798; 세바시 수면 이혼 179,495; Dear Psychology 53,218; TED "How sleep affects your emotions" 344,819).
- **Sub-angle 4 (sleep debt / tired):** strong — 스브스뉴스 weekend-catch-up story (2.62M) is on-topic; EBS 다큐 chronic fatigue (2.18M) is medical; 뇌과학 쓸래요? (1.41M) is about rest/burnout rather than sleep.
- **Sub-angle 5 (night thoughts):** no on-topic video met the threshold in Korean or English. Korean "새벽 감성" explainers found were tiny (≤2,043 views).
